# POL-02 Billing and payments

## Direct debit
Monthly memberships are billed by direct debit on the same day each month as the start date of the membership. Members can pay by bank account or by Visa or Mastercard.

## Failed payments
If a direct debit fails, a failed payment fee of $10 is added and the payment is tried again 3 days later. If the second attempt also fails, the membership is suspended until the overdue amount is paid. Suspended members cannot check in.

## Updating payment details
Members can update their card or bank details in the member app under Account > Payment details. Changes made less than 2 business days before a billing date apply from the following billing date.

## Price changes
Ridgeline gives at least 30 days' notice by email before any change to membership prices.

## Receipts
Tax invoices for every payment are available in the member app under Account > Payment history.

## Payment disputes
Questions about a charge that looks wrong are handled by the membership team, who can see the account. Front desk staff and the online assistant cannot see full card numbers or issue refunds.
