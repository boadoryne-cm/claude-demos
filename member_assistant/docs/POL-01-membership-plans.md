# POL-01 Membership plans and prices

Ridgeline Climbing Co. is a fictional indoor climbing gym in Geelong, Victoria. All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST.

## Plans
- **Casual entry:** $28 per visit. Shoe and harness hire is $8 extra.
- **10-visit pass:** $250. A 10-visit pass is valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.
- **Monthly membership:** $99 per month, paid by direct debit.
- **Annual membership:** $990, paid upfront.

## Joining fee
New monthly members pay a one-off joining fee of $49. The joining fee is waived for annual memberships.

## Concession pricing
Full-time students, holders of a Seniors Card and holders of a Health Care Card get 15% off monthly and annual memberships. A valid card must be shown at the front desk before the discount is applied. The concession discount does not apply to casual entry, 10-visit passes or the joining fee.

## What every membership includes
Unlimited bouldering and roped climbing during opening hours, use of the training area and free use of the auto-belays. Gear hire is not included in any membership.
