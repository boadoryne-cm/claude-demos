# POL-07 Kids and youth

## Under 14
Climbers under 14 must be supervised at all times by a parent or guardian aged 18 or over who stays in the gym. A supervising adult who is not climbing does not pay entry.

## Ages 14 to 17
Climbers aged 14 to 17 can climb without an adult in the gym, once a parent or guardian has signed the waiver and the climber has passed a belay check or the Intro to Bouldering class.

## Youth memberships
Climbers aged 14 to 17 can hold a monthly or annual membership with a parent or guardian as the account holder. Youth memberships get the 15% concession discount.

## Birthday parties
Parties are for ages 6 to 13, run on weekends, and cost $320 for up to 10 children, including gear and a 90-minute session with an instructor. Book through the website at least 14 days ahead. A $100 deposit is non-refundable within 7 days of the party.
