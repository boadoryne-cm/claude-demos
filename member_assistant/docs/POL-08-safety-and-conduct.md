# POL-08 Safety and conduct

## Belay checks
Everyone must pass a belay check with staff before belaying anyone at Ridgeline, even if they have climbed elsewhere. A belay check is free and takes about 10 minutes. The auto-belays do not need a belay check, but climbers must watch the 2-minute safety video first.

## Rules on the wall
- No climbing above someone else on the same wall, or walking under a climber.
- Auto-belays: clip in before you start, and check the clip every time.
- No lead climbing without a lead check.
- Liquid chalk or chalk balls only. Loose chalk is not allowed.

## Injuries
Any injury, however small, must be reported to a staff member on the day so it can be written up in the incident register. The online assistant cannot record injuries.

## Conduct
Staff can ask anyone climbing unsafely or treating others badly to stop climbing or leave. Serious or repeated breaches can lead to the membership being cancelled without a refund.
