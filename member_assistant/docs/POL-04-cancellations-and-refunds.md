# POL-04 Cancellations and refunds

## Monthly memberships
Monthly members can cancel at any time with 30 days' written notice, by email to members@ridgeline.example or in the member app. Billing continues during the notice period. There are no refunds for part months.

## Annual memberships
Annual members have a 7-day cooling-off period from the date of purchase. Cancelling within the cooling-off period gives a full refund, as long as the member has not climbed more than twice.

After the cooling-off period, annual memberships are not refundable, except:
- on medical grounds, with a medical certificate saying the member cannot climb for at least 3 months; or
- if the member moves more than 50 km from the gym, with proof of the new address.

In those cases the unused months are refunded pro rata, less a $50 administration fee. Requests are reviewed by the membership team.

## 10-visit passes and casual entry
10-visit passes and casual entries are non-refundable. A 10-visit pass can be transferred to another person once, at the front desk, with both people present.

## Joining fee
The joining fee is not refundable, except within the cooling-off period for annual members, where no joining fee is charged.
