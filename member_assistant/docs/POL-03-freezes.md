# POL-03 Freezing a membership

## Who can freeze
Monthly and annual members can freeze their membership. 10-visit passes cannot be frozen.

## How long
A freeze must be at least 2 weeks and no longer than 3 months. Members can freeze for a total of up to 3 months in any 12-month period.

## Cost
- **Monthly members:** a freeze costs $5 per week, charged instead of the normal monthly fee.
- **Annual members:** a freeze is free. The end date of the annual membership moves back by the length of the freeze.
- **Medical freezes:** with a medical certificate, a freeze is free for monthly members too, and may last up to 6 months.

## How to request a freeze
Request a freeze in the member app under Account > Freeze, or at the front desk. Requests must be made at least 7 days before the next billing date, or they start from the following billing date.

## Ending a freeze early
Members can end a freeze early at the front desk. Normal billing restarts from that day.
