# POL-06 Classes and bookings

## Classes
- **Intro to Bouldering:** 60 minutes, $35. Free for members once.
- **Learn to Belay:** 90 minutes, $55. Members pay $40. Passing the course counts as a belay check.
- **Lead Climbing course:** 2 sessions of 2 hours, $180. Members pay $150. Must have passed a belay check first.

## Booking
Book classes in the member app or on the website, up to 14 days ahead. Classes run with at least 3 people. If a class is cancelled by Ridgeline, everyone booked gets a full refund or a free move to another date.

## Cancelling a booking
Cancel at least 24 hours before the class for a full refund or credit. Cancellations less than 24 hours before the class get no refund. A member who does not turn up to 3 booked classes within 60 days cannot book classes for 30 days.

## Waitlist
When a class is full, join the waitlist in the app. If a place opens, the next person gets a notification and has 2 hours to accept it.
