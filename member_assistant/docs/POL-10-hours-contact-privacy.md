# POL-10 Opening hours, contact and privacy

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 6 am to 10 pm
- Saturday and Sunday: 8 am to 8 pm
- Public holidays: 9 am to 5 pm
- Closed on Christmas Day and Good Friday

Last entry is 30 minutes before closing.

## Contact
- Membership team: members@ridgeline.example, Monday to Friday 9 am to 5 pm. Replies within 1 business day.
- Front desk phone: (03) 5550 1234 during opening hours.

## Privacy
Ridgeline does not share any information about one member's account with another person, including family members who are not on the same account. Members can ask for a copy of the personal information Ridgeline holds about them by emailing the membership team.
