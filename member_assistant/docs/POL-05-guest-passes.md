# POL-05 Guest passes

## How many
- Monthly members get 2 free guest passes each calendar month.
- Annual members get 4 free guest passes each calendar month.
- Casual climbers and 10-visit pass holders do not get guest passes.

Unused guest passes do not roll over to the next month.

## Rules for guests
- The member must come in with the guest and check in at the same time.
- Every guest must complete the online waiver before climbing.
- Guests aged 14 to 17 need a parent or guardian to sign the waiver. Guests under 14 cannot use a guest pass.
- Guest passes cannot be used on Saturdays between 10 am and 2 pm.
- Guests who want to use ropes must pass a belay check first. Otherwise they can boulder only.
