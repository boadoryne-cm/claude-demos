# POL-09 Member app and account help

## Logging in
Log in with the email address used to join. To reset a password, tap "Forgot password" on the login screen. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. Ridgeline staff will never ask for your password.

## QR check-in not working
If the QR code does not scan at the front desk:
1. Pull down on the home screen to refresh the code. Each code is valid for 60 seconds.
2. Turn the screen brightness up.
3. Check that the app is updated to the latest version.
If it still does not work, the front desk can check you in with photo ID.

## Changing details
Members can change their phone number, emergency contact and payment details in the app under Account. To change the email address on the account, contact the membership team, because the email is used to log in.

## Family accounts
One account holder can manage up to 5 family members. Each family member has their own membership and QR code.

## What the online assistant can and cannot do
The online assistant answers questions about Ridgeline's policies. It cannot see or change member accounts, bookings or payments. For anything about a specific account, it passes the conversation to the membership team.
