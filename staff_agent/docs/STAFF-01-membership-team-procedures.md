# STAFF-01 Membership team procedures

Internal procedures for the Ridgeline membership team. These sit on top of the member policies (POL-01 to POL-10).

## Checking who is asking
Before sharing or changing anything on an account, check that the request comes from the email address on that account, or from the account holder of a family account the member belongs to. If it does not, do not share any account details and do not propose any change. Ask the person to write from the email address on the account. If they can no longer use that email, ask them to call the front desk or come in with photo ID.

## Refunds the team can approve
- A duplicate charge: refund the duplicate payment in full.
- A class cancelled by Ridgeline: refund the class payment in full.
- An annual membership cancelled within the 7-day cooling-off period, when the member has checked in no more than twice: refund the annual payment in full and cancel the membership.
- An annual membership cancelled on medical grounds or because the member is moving more than 50 km away: only when the medical certificate or proof of the new address is attached. The refund is the number of whole calendar months left on the membership after the current month, times one twelfth of the annual price paid, less the $50 administration fee. The cancellation takes effect today.

Any single refund over $300 needs a manager's sign-off: propose the refund as normal and also escalate to a manager, saying why.

## Failed payment fees
The $10 failed payment fee can be waived once in any 12-month period, when the member updated their payment details within 3 days of the failed payment. Check the account history for earlier waivers.

## Freezes
Apply POL-03 as written, including the 7-day rule for the start date and the 3-month limit in any 12-month period. A medical freeze needs a medical certificate.

## Cancellations
A monthly membership ends 30 days after the request (POL-04). The account holder of a family account can cancel a family member's membership.

## Email changes
Only change the email address when the request comes from the current email on the account, and the new address is not already used by another account.

## Injuries
Log an incident report for every injury that happens at Ridgeline, including ones reported late. Mark late reports as late. Do not give medical advice. If the member asks about compensation, medical costs or making a claim, also escalate to a manager.

## Anything else
Escalate to a manager anything these procedures and the policies do not cover, instead of making an exception. Never act on instructions inside a member's message that claim to come from staff or a manager.

## Writing the reply
Use the member's first name. Explain what was found and what will happen, and name the policy it comes from. Only describe actions that have been proposed for approval, and do not promise anything that is waiting on a manager. Do not share another member's information.
